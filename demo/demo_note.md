## Pulse calibration

For an ideal resonant two-level drive, the target rotation is

$$
R_x(\theta)=\exp[-i\theta\sigma_x/2].\tag{1}
$$

The rotation in $\ref{eq:1}$ is the reference operation in this illustrative example [@nielsenQuantumComputationQuantum2010].

As shown in $\ref{fig:1}$, the Gaussian-shaped drive envelope is a schematic pulse, not experimental data.
